Detailed Description

Many independent time-of-flight KMC runs on a ToFGraph and their statistics.

Per run: the time until the first carrier reaches a sink ("first") and the mean transit time per absorbed carrier, transit = absorption - injection time ("transit"); with all carriers injected at t = 0 these are the observables of GraphRandomWalk. Censored runs (stopped by a limit) are counted and left out of the means, which biases them low; the caller should report them.

Transient current (Ramo-Shockley): every accepted hop contributes its displacement along the transport normal, binned on a logarithmic time axis and normalized by L x injected carriers x bin width, so that its integral is the mean displacement per injected carrier in units of L (about 1 for complete collection). The collected fraction is the number of absorptions up to the end of each bin per injected carrier.

Runs are done in blocks in parallel (OpenMP); per-run data are reduced in run order, so results do not depend on the number of threads.
