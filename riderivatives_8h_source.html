20// Contracted RI integrals and integral derivatives for analytic gradients
21// (defined in libint2_derivative_calls.cc). Nothing of size
22// Naux * Nao^2 or natoms * Naux^2 is stored: three-centre integrals and
23// their derivatives are contracted per aux shell as they are computed, the
24// metric derivatives per shell pair.
25//
26// Any RI gradient term of the form
27// sum_P sum_{mu nu} Gamma^P_{mu nu} d(mu nu|P)/dR - 1/2 sum_PQ G_PQ
28// d(P|Q)/dR
29// (RI-J, RI-K, and the screened-exchange terms of BSE gradients) is
30// assembled from ContractThreeCenterDerivatives and
31// ContractCoulombMetricDerivatives.
