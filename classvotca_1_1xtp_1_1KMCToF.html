Detailed Description

Time-of-flight kinetic Monte Carlo: carriers are injected in the source region (one or more pulses), hop with site exclusion and leave at the sink. Records per run the first arrival and the mean transit time per carrier (the observables "first" and "transit" of grw) and the transient current (Ramo-Shockley) and collected fraction on a logarithmic time axis.
