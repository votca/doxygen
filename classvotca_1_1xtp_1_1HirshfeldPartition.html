Full d(Tr[D*W_c])/dR for all atoms of mol: the sum of the four product-rule terms of d[omega_p * w_c(r_p) * rho_molecule(r_p)]/dR (SSW grid weight, weight function, Pulay, grid-point translation). Isolated-atom reference density at point, rho_i^free(point) = sum_munu P_munu phi_mu(point) phi_nu(point), with atom_basis centered on the atom in the molecule.

All shells are evaluated (AOShell::EvalAOspace) without the significant-shell screening of GridBox, which does not pay off for a single-atom basis.

Gradient grad_r w_target(point) with respect to the evaluation point (EvaluateWeightGradient differentiates with respect to an atom position instead). Needed for the grid-point-translation force term: grid points move rigidly with their owner atom (r_p = R_owner + const, see PulayGradient), so quantities evaluated at r_p depend on R_owner through r_p.

grad_r w(r) = [grad_r rho_target(r) - w(r) * grad_r rho_tot(r)] / rho_tot(r)

static

CDFT force term from the derivative of the SSW grid weights, d(Tr[D*W_c])/dR for all atoms of mol (Natoms x 3). The trace is contracted inside the grid loop, without forming d(W_c)/dR, as in Vxc_Potential::GridWeightGradient. Implemented separately from that function (duplicating the SSW functions) to leave the validated XC gradient code unchanged.

density_matrix is the converged molecular density in the full AO basis, from which rho_molecule(point) is evaluated; w_c(point) comes from the Hirshfeld references in atoms.

Pulay (basis-function derivative) and grid-point-translation terms of d(Tr[D*W_c])/dR (Natoms x 3), computed in one grid loop. The translation term arises because grid points move with their owner atom (see EvaluatePointWeightGradient).

Follows the bookkeeping and sign convention of PulayGradient, -weight * potential * temp(mu) * ao.derivatives.row(mu), with w_c(point) in place of xc.df_drho; kept separate from it for the same reason as GridWeightDerivativeContribution.
