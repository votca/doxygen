20// Hirshfeld real-space partitioning, used as the weight function for CDFT
21// charge constraints. Hirshfeld is preferred over Becke/SSW partitioning,
22// which gives unphysical atomic charges for atoms of very different size
23// (cf. CP2K).
24//
25// The weight of atom i is w_i(r) = rho_i^free(r) / sum_j rho_j^free(r),
26// where rho_i^free is the spherically averaged density of the isolated
27// atom (DFTEngine::ComputeHirshfeldReferenceDensities). The reference
28// densities are passed in explicitly; as in DFTGradient, all methods are
29// static and do not access DFTEngine internals. Each reference density is
30// evaluated in its own single-atom basis centered on the atom, so no
31// embedding into the molecular AO basis is needed.
