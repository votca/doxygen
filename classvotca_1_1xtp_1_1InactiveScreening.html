 Poles in the form of transitions that reproduce a response at zero and at one imaginary frequency: Y(i w) = sum_k 4 W_k/(W_k^2 + w^2) c_k c_k^T, exactly at w = 0 and w = w_i on the range of Y(0). With Y(0) = G G^T (eigenvectors above tolerance times the largest eigenvalue) and G^+ Y(i w_i) G^+T = Q diag(l) Q^T, l_k = W_k^2/(W_k^2 + w_i^2) and c_k = sqrt(W_k / 4) (G Q)_k. For a genuine sum of transitions every l lies in (0, 1); for a difference of two responses (exact mode) it may not, and such modes keep only their static response (a pole at 1e3 Ha; counted). Detailed Description

Static screening by the inactive region of a PbE calculation.

In the auxiliary frame of the stored three-centre integrals (M = (mn|Q) T, T T^T = V^-1) the static RPA dielectric matrix is 1 + X with X = sum_ia 4/(e_a - e_i) M_ia M_ia^T. Dressing the active integrals with S = (1 + R)^(1/2), R = (1 + Y)^-1 - 1, for a positive semidefinite Y gives S (1 + S X_A S)^-1 S = (1 + X_A + Y)^-1. With Y = X_full - X_A ("exact") this is the static screened interaction of the full calculation; with Y = X_B over the semicanonical inactive orbitals ("block") it is that up to the active-inactive Fock coupling. R enters GW and BSE like the reaction field of a polarizable environment (GW::setReactionField, BSE::setReactionField), and combines with one: 1 + R = ((1 + R_env)^-1 + Y)^-1, the Dyson equation in which the inactive region and the environment polarize each other.

Y at several imaginary frequencies, as GWBSE uses it: one integral pass for the full (exact) or inactive (block) occupied orbitals, with the full calculation's virtuals inside the RPA range (homo+1..rpamax; the inactive levels the projector shifted above them are left out). In exact mode X_A comes from active, the integrals GW holds (rpa_energies for levels rpamin..rpamax, excluded holes left out as in the RPA), so the active integrals are not built a second time. The virtual_cutoff > 0 (Ha above the LUMO) keeps only the virtuals up to it, in both terms of exact mode. The static point is the imaginary frequency eta, as RPA::calculate_epsilon_r at 0 (identical weights).

X = sum_ia 4/(e_a - e_i) M_ia M_ia^T over the given occupied and virtual orbitals, in the frame TCMatrix_gwbse::Fill stores its integrals in for auxbasis.

The occupied orbitals are processed in chunks of at most chunk_bytes of integrals, so the full tensor is never stored. With imaginary_frequency w > 0 the weights are 4D/(D^2 + w^2), the response at i w as RPA::calculate_epsilon_i builds it.
