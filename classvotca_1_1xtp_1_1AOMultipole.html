enum class votca::xtp::AOMultipole::Moments
strong

Which moments of the external sites enter FillPotential. The potential is linear in the moments, so Permanent + Induced equals All.

Enumerator
All 

permanent moments plus induced dipoles

Permanent 

permanent moments only (charge, dipole, quadrupole)

Induced 

induced dipoles only; sites without one are skipped
