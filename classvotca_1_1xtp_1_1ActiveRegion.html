Detailed Description

Checks and grows the active region of a projection-based embedding from the frontier orbitals of the full reference calculation.

Atoms are indexed by their position in Orbitals::QMAtoms(); the dft_in_dft.activeatoms indices are these positions.

Without a truncated basis the embedded virtual space is the full one, so only the occupied frontier orbitals decide whether an excitation fits into the active region (its holes must lie there). With a truncated basis the virtual frontier orbitals count as well.
