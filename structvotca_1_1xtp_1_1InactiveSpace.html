Detailed Description

Occupied orbitals of the inactive region of a projection-based embedding, with the virtual space they are excited into.

The occupied orbitals are the inactive part of the occupied space of the full reference calculation, brought into semicanonical form (the Fock operator is diagonal within them). The embedded active orbitals are the semicanonical orbitals of the complement (eigenfunctions of Q F_full Q), so F_full still couples active and inactive occupied orbitals, and the response summed over the two blocks separately differs from the full one at first order in that coupling. The virtual orbitals are the full reference virtuals, equal to the embedded virtuals at convergence.
