Detailed Description

A response the RPA adds to its own sum over these integrals, epsilon(w) = 1 + X[M](w) + F^T Y(w) F, given in the fill-time frame (F = AuxFrame()) at fixed frequencies: the static response of a part of the system that is not in M, e.g. the inactive region of a projection-based embedding (InactiveScreening). Survives Fill and Rebuild (same auxiliary basis, same metric). The RPA throws when it is asked for epsilon at a frequency that has no Y.
