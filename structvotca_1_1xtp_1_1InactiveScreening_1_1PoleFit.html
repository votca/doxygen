Detailed Description

Poles in the form of transitions that reproduce a response at zero and at one imaginary frequency: Y(i w) = sum_k 4 W_k/(W_k^2 + w^2) c_k c_k^T, exactly at w = 0 and w = w_i on the range of Y(0). With Y(0) = G G^T (eigenvectors above tolerance times the largest eigenvalue) and G^+ Y(i w_i) G^+T = Q diag(l) Q^T, l_k = W_k^2/(W_k^2 + w_i^2) and c_k = sqrt(W_k / 4) (G Q)_k. For a genuine sum of transitions every l lies in (0, 1); for a difference of two responses (exact mode) it may not, and such modes keep only their static response (a pole at 1e3 Ha; counted).
