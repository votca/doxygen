Detailed Description

Random numbers for kinetic Monte Carlo: one independent stream per (seed, stream) pair. std::seed_seq and mt19937_64 are fully specified by the standard and the uniform numbers are built from the raw bits, so a stream is identical with every standard library (unlike std::uniform_real_distribution / std::normal_distribution).
