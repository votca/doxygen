Detailed Description

Coupling of the QM region with Gaussian charges and dipoles (the induced charges and dipoles of charge-response sites).

A site k of width s carries the charge density q g_k(r) + mu . (2 beta) (r - R_k) g_k(r), g_k(r) = (beta/pi)^3/2 exp(-beta |r - R_k|^2), beta = 1/s^2, i.e. a normalized Gaussian charge and the Gaussian dipole -mu . grad g_k (dipole moment mu). The integrals are three-centre Coulomb integrals of an AO pair with an s and a p primitive at R_k (libint2), so the QM->site potential and the site->QM Hamiltonian use the same integrals and the coupling is exactly symmetric. Width 0 is not allowed (use AOMultipole for points).
