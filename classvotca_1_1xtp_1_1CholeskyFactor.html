Detailed Description

Cholesky factor A = L L^T of a symmetric positive definite matrix, with the same backends as SymmetricEigen (Accelerate LAPACK on macOS, LAPACKE with MKL or Accelerate, otherwise Eigen). ok() is false if A is not positive definite.
