Detailed Description

Kinetic Monte Carlo of carriers (excitons) that hop and decay with a site-dependent rate, at constant number: every decayed carrier is replaced at once by a new one on a random free injection site (calculator kmclifetime).

Each run starts with the carriers on distinct random injection sites and stops after its share of the requested decays. Per decay: lifetime (decay - injection time), displacement (unwrapped), hops and decay site. Statistics are over all decays; their standard errors assume independent decays (exact for one carrier). Also the occupation probability of every site (summed over carriers) and the fraction of decays per site. Runs are done in parallel and reduced in run order.

Exact(): for one carrier the same averages without sampling, from linear systems with A = K + diag(W) - W (K: decay rates, W: hop rates): A tau = 1 (lifetime), A m = b with b_i = sum_j w_ij R_ij (mean displacement), A s = sum_j w_ij (R_ij^2 + 2 R_ij m_j) (mean squared displacement, per component), and A^T g = p0 for the expected time per site (occupation) and the decay probabilities k_j g_j, all averaged over a uniform start on the injection sites.
