Detailed Description

Induced charges and dipoles of a polar region with charge-response segments (Thole segments may be mixed in).

Unknowns x = (dq of the charge-response sites, mu of all sites), sources c = (-phi, E) from the potentials and fields accumulated in the sites (Phi + Phi_noE, -(V + V_noE)), M = blockdiag(Pi of each charge-response segment, alpha of each site). With T the couplings of the induced charges and dipoles ((T x)_q = induced potential, (T x)_mu = minus the induced field; nothing inside a charge-response segment, the Thole couplings inside a Thole segment):

x = M (c - T x) <=> (1 + M^1/2 T M^1/2) y = M^1/2 c, x = M^1/2 y,

solved by conjugate gradients on the symmetric operator. The null space of Pi (charge conservation) is handled by M^1/2. The kernels are those of eeInteractor (FillDipoleInteraction, GaussianKernel).
