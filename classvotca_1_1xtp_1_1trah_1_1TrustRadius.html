Detailed Description

Fletcher's trust-radius update. Returns whether the step is accepted (actual and predicted energy changes of the same sign, or both below noise_floor in magnitude) and adjusts the radius: x0.7 for a rejected step or r <= 0.25, x1.2 (up to max_radius) for r > 0.75, with r = actual / predicted.
