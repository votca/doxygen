Detailed Description

Kinetic Monte Carlo of carriers with site exclusion on a ToFGraph.

Every carrier has its own clock: the pluggable WaitingTimeStrategy gives the time of its next candidate event and, at that time, the hop (KMCWaitingTime.h has the exactness argument). The engine processes the earliest pending event (a min-heap over carriers, merged with the scheduled injections); a candidate hop into an occupied site, or a strategy returning -1, is a null event: time has advanced, the carrier draws a new candidate time. Exclusion therefore never shortens waiting times. Only the clock of the acting carrier changes at an event.

Strategies: ExponentialWaitingTime (static rates), ThinningWaitingTime (time-dependent rates with upper bounds), and the template IntegratedRateInversion (periodic rates).

Carriers enter at scheduled injection times on a free connected source site (uniform) and leave at a sink. Optionally they also decay with a per-site rate (setDecay): a second, exponential clock competing with the hops, redrawn after every event of the carrier (exact, as the decay is memoryless); a decayed carrier can be replaced at once by a new one on a free source site (constant number of carriers). Observers receive every injection, accepted hop, absorption and decay.

Runs are independent and reproducible: the random numbers of a run depend only on (seed, run index), not on threads or the order of runs.
