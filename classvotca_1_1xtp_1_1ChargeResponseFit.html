Detailed Description

Parametrization of a charge-response molecule (see ChargeResponse) from the static density response of a DFT ground state.

The perturbations are the Hirshfeld-localized potentials w_k (one per atom; sum_k w_k = 1) and three uniform fields. With the AO matrices W_k of the weights and the first-order density matrices dP of the perturbations (electron potential energy +w_k, resp. +r_c, per unit strength):

Pi_jk = -Tr(W_j dP_k) (dq = -Pi phi) alpha_ac = -Tr(r_a dP_c) (molecular polarizability) alpha^res = alpha - X^T Pi X (X: positions - centre)

The residual polarizability is distributed over the atoms as one tensor scaled by the traces of the Hirshfeld atomic polarizabilities alpha_k,ac = -int w_k (r - R_k)_a drho_c. Fit obtains dP by central finite differences of xtpdft SCFs; the partition integrals and the analysis are separate for testing.

The fit with xtpdft (dftoptions: a dftpackage tree with name xtp): the ground state of orb (atoms, charge and spin set; closed shell), which orb holds on return, then the first-order densities of all perturbations, analytically or as dP = (P(+step) - P(-step)) / (2 step) from SCFs warm-started from the ground state. The Hirshfeld references are xtpdft's (DFTEngine::HirshfeldReferences).
