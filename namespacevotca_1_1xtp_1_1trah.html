Detailed Description

Trust-region augmented-Hessian (TRAH) step for orbital rotations, after Helmich-Paris, J. Chem. Phys. 154, 164104 (2021).

Shared by the closed-shell (ConvergenceAcc) and unrestricted (UKSConvergenceAcc) SCF. Orbital rotations are parametrised as C' = C exp(kappa) with kappa_ia = x_ia (i occupied, a virtual) and kappa_ai = -x_ia; the vector x is flattened as x(i * n_virt + a), with the channels of an unrestricted calculation stacked (alpha first). The caller supplies the energy gradient g = dE/dx, the action of the energy Hessian d^2E/dx dx on a block of vectors, and a positive diagonal estimate of the Hessian for preconditioning.

The step minimises the quadratic model g.x + 1/2 x.H.x within |x| <= trust radius. It is the lowest eigenvector (mu, [v0; v]) of the scaled augmented Hessian [[0, a g^T], [a g, H]], x = v / (a v0), which satisfies (H - mu) x = -g. All a are handled in one Davidson subspace: only the first row and column of the projected matrix depend on a, so searching a costs no Hessian products. a = 1 is used when its step lies inside the trust radius; otherwise a is increased until |x| lies in [lower_fraction, 1] x trust radius.
