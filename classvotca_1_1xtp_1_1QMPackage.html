virtual void votca::xtp::QMPackage::setSCFToleranceFloor ( double ,
double  )
inlinevirtual

Lower bounds for the SCF convergence thresholds (energy in Hartree, DIIS error) of the next run; the configured thresholds apply where they are looser. Used by QM/MM, whose outer loop does not need the inner SCF converged further than it resolves itself. Only xtp uses it.
