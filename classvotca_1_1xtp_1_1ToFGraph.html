Detailed Description

Hopping graph for time-of-flight (ToF) transport.

Built from a periodic morphology (segments, box, neighbour list with couplings). The periodicity is cut across one box vector (the transport axis): pairs that connect two segments through a periodic image along that vector are dropped, the other two directions stay periodic. Source and sink sites are selected by slabs at the two faces normal to the transport axis and/or by segment type patterns. Rates are Marcus rates from Rate_Engine at the given temperature and field.

The graph is the common input of the ToF kinetic Monte Carlo and of the graph random walk (hitting time) calculations, so both see exactly the same sites, edges and rates.

With Options::bulk nothing is cut, the periodicity is kept in all three directions and there are no sinks: the graph of the bulk KMC (kmcmultiple). The ToF methods (GraphRandomWalk, ToFSimulation) refuse such a graph; a ToF graph stays periodic in the two directions normal to the transport axis. The source sites are then the injection sites (source_pattern, all sites if empty); normal, thickness, length and the coordinate x are zero. In this mode a field makes U = E - q F.r multivalued (it changes by q F.(box vector) around the torus), so the rates satisfy detailed balance only without a field component along a box vector, and the potential is only informative.

Segments whose type matches one of Options::ignore_types take no part in transport: they keep their index but have no edges and are neither source nor sink.

Coordinates along the transport axis are measured along the unit normal of the cut face, from the face at fractional coordinate 0 (source side) to the one at 1 (sink side). All quantities are in atomic units, rates in 1/s.
