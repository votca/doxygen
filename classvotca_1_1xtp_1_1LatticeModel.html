Detailed Description

Surrogate lattice model of a hopping system, written as an ordinary XTP topology (segments with one pseudo atom each, a periodic box and a neighbour list with couplings), so that every transport method in XTP can be run and cross-checked on it.

Follows the setup of Chen, van der Hoorn, Baumeier, arXiv:2405.15836: simple cubic lattice with spacing a, sites at (i+1/2)a, periodic in all three directions (a time-of-flight calculation cuts one of them), pairs for |r| < cutoff, |J|^2 = J0^2 exp(-(|r|-a)/decay), one reorganization energy for all pairs, and Gaussian site energies without or with spatial correlation (moving-average construction of Baumeier et al., PRB 86, 184202 (2012); eq. 2.5 of the paper excludes the site itself from the averages, see Options::include_self).

All quantities are in atomic units (bohr, Hartree).
