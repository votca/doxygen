Detailed Description

What GWBSE needs to add the static screening of the inactive region: the mode and the orbitals of the full reference calculation that enter the response (see InactiveScreening::Prepare).

  • block: the semicanonical inactive occupied orbitals (InactiveSpace); Y = X_B. Misses the terms of first order in the Fock coupling between active and inactive occupied orbitals; cost proportional to the number of inactive orbitals.
  • exact: all canonical occupied orbitals of the full calculation; Y = X_full - X_A, with X_A from the embedded active orbitals. Gives the static screened interaction of the full calculation exactly; cost proportional to all occupied orbitals.
