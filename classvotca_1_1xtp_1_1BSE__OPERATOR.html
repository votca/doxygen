void votca::xtp::BSE_OPERATOR< cqp, cx, cd, cd2 >::set_direct_cache ( std::shared_ptr< SymmetricTiles > cache)
inline

Keep the screened direct term (Hd for cd, Hd2 for cd2; unit prefactor) in this matrix, which is built on first use unless it already holds it. Operators of the same BSE can share it (e.g. the singlet and triplet A, or Hd for the analysis); null disables it.

Without the dense cache the screened direct term is applied in large products, one block of Hamiltonian rows (all v1 for one c1) at a time; true selects the row-by-row kernel (one Hamiltonian row at a time).
