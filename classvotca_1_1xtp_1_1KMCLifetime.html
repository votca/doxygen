Detailed Description

Kinetic Monte Carlo of excitons (or other carriers) with site-dependent lifetimes in a periodic box: lifetime, distance travelled and diffusion length, where the excitons decay, site occupations (LifetimeSimulation on a bulk ToFGraph with KMCEngine). For one exciton also the exact averages without sampling.
