Detailed Description

The screening W_c = W - v in modes s, for the self-energy dump. Frame: the eigenvectors phi_i of eps^-1(0) in the auxiliary basis of the integrals (the plasmon-pole frame); W_c in it, eps^-1_ii(i nu) - 1 = - sum_s a_is Omega_s / (nu^2 + Omega_s^2), with a_is = 4 (phi_i^T Z_s)^2 (exact; Z_s the aux vector of RPA mode s) or a_ii = w_i Omega_i (plasmon pole: one pole per mode).
