Detailed Description

template<class Grid>
struct votca::xtp::Vxc_Potential< Grid >::FXCKernel

The ground-state part of IntegrateFXC, for many responses at one density (e.g. the iterations of a CPKS solve): per grid point that passes the density screening, the weight, the density gradient and the second derivatives of libxc (about 70 bytes per point). Saves the density, its gradient and libxc in every application; the AO values are recomputed (caching them took 1.4 GB for pyridine + 6 H2O / def2-SVP and saved only 10 %).
