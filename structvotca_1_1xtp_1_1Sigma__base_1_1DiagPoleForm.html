Detailed Description

Pole form of the diagonal correlation self-energy (ppm and exact): Sigma_c,nn(w) = prefactor sum_{m,s} c_s r_n(m,s)^2 f(w - z_ms), z_ms = e_m - Omega_s (m occupied), e_m + Omega_s (m virtual), f(t) = t / (t^2 + eta^2), with r_n = residues (B x S, column-major), c_s = mode_factors (nullptr: all 1). The references point into the evaluator and the RPA; they are valid until the next PrepareScreening or RPA energy update.
