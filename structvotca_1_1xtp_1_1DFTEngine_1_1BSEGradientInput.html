Detailed Description

Input of BSEExcitationGradient: one BSE state and the orbital energies its Hamiltonian was built with. The energies are taken as KS energies plus geometry-independent shifts (fixed QP corrections): e_p(R) = eps_p(R) + (energies_bse(p) - eps_p) at the reference.

◆ qp_levels

std::vector<Index> votca::xtp::DFTEngine::BSEGradientInput::qp_levels

If not empty: instead of the BSE excitation energy (X, Y and singlet are ignored), the gradients of the energies of these levels (absolute MO indices in vmin..cmax): QP energies in the QP window with qp_response, otherwise the energies entering as energies_bse (KS plus fixed shifts). KS-degenerate levels: gradient of their mean.

◆ qp_response

bool votca::xtp::DFTEngine::BSEGradientInput::qp_response = false

Response of the QP corrections (G0W0 with the plasmon-pole model): the BSE energies of the levels qpmin..qpmax are then solutions of the QP equation E = eps + (1 - a_x) Sigma_x - v_xc + Sigma_c(E) with the screening and the poles of G at energies_rpa (KS energies plus fixed shifts), and their derivative is included. Otherwise all energies are KS energies plus fixed shifts.
